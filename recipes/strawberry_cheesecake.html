Strawberry Cheesecake

Description

A cheesecake rich enough that additions are not necessary.

Ingredients

  • Cheesecake biscuit base and walls
  • Cheesecake filling
  • Four ingredient strawberry sauce

Steps

  1. Bash biscuits into crumbs, stir in melted butter. Any plain bicuit goes
  2. Filling. Cream cheese, sugar, lemon zest, vanilla, a tiny amount of flour and eggs
  3. Strawberry topping. Strawberries, sugar, cornflour or cornstarch, lemon juice, and vanilla. Throw in saucepan, simmer for 10 minutes, stir through halved strawberries, cool then spread on cheesecake.
